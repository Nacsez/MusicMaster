Music Mastering Tools - Windows quick start

Requires Windows 10/11 on Intel/AMD 64-bit and a browser.
Python and the mastering runtime are included. No installer or administrator
rights are needed. This candidate is unsigned.

1. Extract the ZIP, open the windows folder, and double-click
   MusicMasteringTools.exe. Your default browser opens the workbench.
2. In Library, add your own mixes and reference tracks. The application keeps
   their locations; it does not copy your source audio into its library.
3. In Master, choose the tracks, references, and delivery folder. Remember saves
   that output location for future launches. Check setup, test, then render.
4. Review masters to compare original and completed versions.
5. Close the last workbench tab to exit. Refreshing or minimizing keeps the app
   running. Any active render finishes safely before automatic shutdown.

Your library, preferences, and logs persist separately from the executable:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\MusicMasteringTools\workspace

Each Windows account has its own library. Replacing or moving the executable
preserves that account's library. If you move an audio file, verify/relink its
location in Library. Show in folder opens the exact cataloged location.

Session Logs are available in Activity / System. If startup fails, inspect the
workspace logs folder. Logs can include local paths; keep them private.

WAV mastering is the tested baseline. Browser playback depends on its codecs;
some other input formats require a separately installed FFmpeg decoder.

Source, library recovery, deployment, and build instructions:
